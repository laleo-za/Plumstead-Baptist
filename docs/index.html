Who we are

Our church exalts Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour and we proclaim this glorious Truth to all.

Plumstead Baptist Church is a local Bible-believing church in Cape Town, South Africa, shaped by the Gospel and committed to worship, teaching, prayer, and community. On Sundays we gather at 10:00 as the redeemed people of God to pray, hear the Word preached, and worship our risen Saviour. Throughout the week we meet in smaller groups, serve our city, and encourage one another to follow Jesus in everyday life.

We look forward to welcoming you to our church!
